About

Honest reflection, and then something you can do with it.

I'm an Egyptian-American counseling psychologist and organizational therapist. My work is about helping you see yourself clearly, your gifts and the parts of you that haven't had room to grow, and then doing something real with it.

I believe people are at their best when they're truly seen for who they are and their core spirit. I'm not a sterile, neutral presence across a screen; I'm a human being, rich in life, who will witness you on your path. And this isn't only a place to vent. I take an active role in getting you the outcomes you came for, and I ask the questions that actually move things. Being human in the room never means winging it: everything I do is grounded in real clinical training, evidence-based methods, and the ethics of the profession.

I work across four languages with expats, leaders, and people building lives far from where they started, and I've trained more than 200 people inside organizations. Underneath all of it, I believe in agency, freedom, and living from the heart.

Good to know

A few questions people usually have.

Does online therapy actually work? +

Yes. For most people it's just as effective as meeting in person, and it means we can work together no matter where you are, in a setting where you already feel at home.

Which languages can we work in? +

Arabic, English, Spanish, and Portuguese. Many of the people I work with are living between cultures, and being able to switch languages mid-thought matters.

Where are you based, and can we work across time zones? +

I work with people internationally, including expats and folks building lives far from where they started. We'll find a time that fits your week.

Is it confidential? +

Yes. Our sessions are private and held over secure video. What you bring stays between us, within the standard limits of professional practice.

How do I start? +

Book a one-hour consult using the button at the top or bottom of this page. We'll talk, see how it feels, and go from there.
